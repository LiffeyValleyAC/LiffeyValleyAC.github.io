---
title: Biofreeze Irish Runner 10 Mile Road Championships.
location: Phoenix Park, Dublin
---

<p>
  Well done to all Liffey Valley AC members who took part in the
  <strong>Biofreeze Irish Runner 10 Mile Road Championships 2026</strong>,
  held on <strong>19th July</strong> along Chesterfield Avenue in the Phoenix
  Park.
</p>

<p>
  The club was well represented across the field, with <strong>Simon Monds</strong>
  leading the team home in <strong>1:01:12</strong>, followed by strong runs
  from <strong>Bernard O'Sullivan</strong> in <strong>1:09:26</strong>,
  <strong>Patrick Doyle</strong> and <strong>Dearbhaile Rooney</strong> both in
  <strong>1:12:58</strong>, and <strong>Joseph Whittle</strong> in
  <strong>1:12:42</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  Great performances also came from <strong>Siobhan Nugent</strong>,
  <strong>Michael Ferreira</strong>, <strong>Margaret O'Friel</strong>, and
  <strong>Hilary McDonnell</strong>. Congratulations to everyone who raced and
  represented the club so well at the National 10 Mile Championships.
</p>
