A great morning of racing at the <strong>Rathfarnham 5k Road Race</strong> 🙂🏃‍♂️🏃‍♀️<br><br>

There was a fantastic atmosphere around Rathfarnham, with some very fast racing and a strong turnout from <strong>Liffey Valley AC</strong>. The race also incorporated the <strong>Dublin 5k Championships</strong>, and it proved to be a very successful morning for the club. 💛💙<br><br>

🏆 <strong>Dublin Championship Medal Success</strong><br>
<div style="text-align: center;">
<span style="font-size: 2.6em;" aria-label="Silver medal">🥈</span> <br>
Our <strong>Masters Men's Team</strong> produced a brilliant team performance to claim <strong>silver medals in the Dublin 5k Championships team race</strong>.<br>

<span style="font-size: 2.6em;" aria-label="Silver medal">🥈🥈</span> <br>
<strong>David Carroll</strong> and <strong>Pierce Geoghegan</strong> also enjoyed individual success, both finishing in <strong>silver medal positions in their respective Dublin M35+ and M40+ Championship categories</strong>.
</div>
