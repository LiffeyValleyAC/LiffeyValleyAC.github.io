---
title: Club 5k & 10k Road Championships and George Harrison and Myles Cullen Trophies.
---

<p>The George Harrison and Myles Cullen Memorial Races took place on Sunday, 6 September 2026, at The Acres in the Phoenix Park.</p>

<p><b>Myles Cullen</b> was the founding chairperson of Liffey Valley AC and was integral to the founding of the club. The Myles Cullen Memorial Trophy is awarded to the winner of the closed handicap 10km race, which consists of approximately 3.8 laps around The Acres.</p>

<p><b>George Harrison</b> played a key role in the development of the women's section of Liffey Valley AC. The George Harrison Trophy is awarded to the winner of the closed handicap 5km race, which consists of approximately 1.9 laps around The Acres.</p>

<p>Congratulations to this year's trophy winners, <b>🥇 Arnaud Benjacar</b>, winner of the <b>Myles Cullen Cup</b>, and <b>🥇 Clare Kavanagh</b>, winner of the <b>George Harrison</b> Cup.</p>

<p>Well done to all our athletes for taking part. Thank you, as always, to our officials for organising the races and to our volunteers for their support.</p>
