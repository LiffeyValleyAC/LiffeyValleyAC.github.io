title: Dublin Graded Meeting 8 of 8
location: Morton Stadium, Dublin
---

A huge thank you to all <b>Liffey Valley Athletic Club</b> athletes who competed at the eighth and final Graded Meeting of the 2026 Track Season at <b>Morton Stadium</b> on <b>Wednesday 29/07/26</b>. The final race of the series brought the season to a close with the <b>1500m</b> on the programme.<br>

Well done to <b>Richard Finegan</b> and <b>James Foley</b>, who both represented the club strongly in the <b>1500m Men C</b> race. It was a fitting finish to a busy summer of graded racing, with Liffey Valley AC athletes competing consistently across the full series from May through July.<br>

Congratulations to everyone who raced, supported, coached, paced, travelled, and helped make the 2026 track season such a positive one for the club. A special thanks as always to <b>Jim Davis</b> and <b>Eddie McGrath</b> for their continued support throughout the season.<br>

Well done to everyone involved. See you all back on the track soon.
