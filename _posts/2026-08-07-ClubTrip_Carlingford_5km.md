---
title: Club Trip 2026 - Carlingford 5K
location: Carlingford, Co. Louth, Ireland
---

<p>
  On <strong>7th August at 8:00 PM</strong>, several Liffey Valley AC members
  took part in the <strong>5KM Road Race in Carlingford, Co. Louth</strong>,
  a fantastic event with over <strong>600 participants</strong> on a beautiful
  course along the coast of this lovely village, finishing in the town centre
  after passing through a distinctive tunnel/arch.
</p>

<p>
  More than <strong>16 club members</strong> gathered for the weekend at the
  <strong>Carrickdale Hotel &amp; Spa</strong>, where we relaxed, shared great
  moments, and enjoyed excellent weather. The trip also included activities
  such as hiking at nearby <strong>Slieve Gullion</strong>, adding even more
  memories to a wonderful weekend.
</p>

<p>
  Another successful <strong>club trip</strong>, thanks to the excellent
  organisation by <strong>Ciaran and company</strong>.
</p>
