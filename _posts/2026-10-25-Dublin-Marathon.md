---
title: Club Results at Dublin Marathon 2026
location: Dublin
---

A huge congratulations to all <b>14 Liffey Valley AC athletes</b> who took on the <b>45th Irish Life Dublin Marathon</b> on <b>Sunday, 25th October 2026</b>!<br><br>

Over <b>19,100 runners</b> lined up for the 42.2 km challenge, with the race starting at 8:45 AM. Conditions were challenging, with a cold and windy morning, strong gusty westerly winds and occasional showers, while temperatures reached around 10–12°C during the day. Despite the weather, thousands of enthusiastic spectators lined Dublin's streets, creating another unforgettable marathon atmosphere!<br><br>

Leading the way for <b>Liffey Valley AC</b> was <b>Simon Monds</b>, delivering a fantastic sub-three-hour performance in <b>2:57:35</b>! He was closely followed by <b>Jen Preston</b>, our first female finisher in an impressive <b>3:00:38</b>, and <b>Neil Duffy</b>, who crossed the line in <b>3:01:25</b>.<br><br>

Other strong performances included <b>Jason McGlone (3:10:07)</b>, <b>Michael Ferreira (3:17:24)</b>, <b>Katie Barry (3:18:32)</b>, <b>Patrick Doyle (3:27:16)</b> and <b>Conor McAvoy (3:30:59)</b>.<br><br>

Congratulations also to <b>Orla Gordon, Keela Farnon, Siobhan Nugent, Bébhinn Ní Chiosáin, Aoife Brady and John Tuohy</b>, who showed tremendous determination to complete the marathon and proudly represent the club!<br><br>

At the front of the race, <b>Ethiopia's Abay Alemu Lesa</b> claimed the men's title in an outstanding <b>2:10:28</b>, while fellow Ethiopian <b>Zenebu Fikdau Jebesa</b> won the women's race in <b>2:24:26</b>, setting a new personal best. The Irish National Marathon Championship titles went to <b>Hugh Armstrong (2:14:51)</b> and <b>Judith Storm (2:35:46)</b>.<br><br>

A brilliant day for Irish athletics and another fantastic showing from <b>Liffey Valley AC</b>! Whether chasing a personal best, breaking the three-hour barrier or simply taking on the marathon challenge, every finisher deserves enormous credit for their dedication, hard work and determination.<br><br>

<b>Well done to everyone who represented the club, and a special thank you to all the supporters who cheered our runners along the course!</b> 💚🏃‍♂️🏃‍♀️
